$$\frac{\partial}{\partial t}\int_{V_{0}}\rho\,\mathrm{d}V=-\oint_{\partial V_{0}}\rho\boldsymbol{u}\cdot\mathrm{d}\boldsymbol{A}$$

$$\int_{V_{0}}\frac{\partial\rho}{\partial t}\,\mathrm{d}V=-\int_{V_{0}}\nabla\cdot(\rho\boldsymbol{u})\,\mathrm{d}V$$

$$\frac{\partial\rho}{\partial t}+\nabla\cdot(\rho\boldsymbol{u})=0$$
